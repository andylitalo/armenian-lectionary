## CASING

Not wrong data, but not a *registered* difference either: `canonical_commem` folds `PRESENTATION OF OUR LORD TO THE TEMPLE` and says nothing about these. Decide explicitly — either register the fold in `dev/source_corrections` or preserve the source's casing — so the raw-string oracle has no unexplained residue.
